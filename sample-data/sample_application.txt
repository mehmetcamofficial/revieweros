Project Title: MedLens AI

Problem:
Hospitals struggle to detect early-stage cancer from fragmented clinical data.

Solution:
We propose a machine learning platform that analyzes imaging metadata, lab results, and clinical notes to identify high-risk patients.

Innovation:
The system combines multimodal data fusion and explainable AI.

Market:
Target customers are private hospitals and diagnostic centers.

Timeline:
The team plans to complete clinical validation in 3 months.

Budget:
The requested budget is $500,000.

Team:
The team includes two AI engineers and one business developer.

Ethics:
The proposal mentions data privacy but does not provide a detailed consent or anonymization plan.
